\noindent\textbf{Data Availability Statement.}
The publicly available Visual Genome dataset can be accessed at
\url{https://visualgenome.org/api/v0}. The SpokenCOCO speech recordings
are available from the MIT Spoken Audio Captioning Corpora at
\url{https://sls.csail.mit.edu/downloads/placesaudio/downloads.cgi},
and Spoken ObjectNet is available at
\url{https://sls.csail.mit.edu/downloads/son/index.cgi}.
The corresponding MS COCO and ObjectNet images are available from
\url{https://cocodataset.org/} and \url{https://objectnet.dev/download.html},
respectively.
